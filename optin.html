This is a public copy of the SMS opt-in screen inside the Pete Ryan Slack Alerts app (the real settings screen is only visible after signing in to the app). Text me about Slack messages

Pete Ryan Slack Alerts watches the Slack account you connected to this app. When that account gets a new direct message or @mention, the app texts you a short alert so you know someone is trying to reach you when you are away from your computer.


SMS alerts enabled. A confirmation text is on its way.

What happens after you opt in

Right after you check the box and tap Yes, enable SMS alerts, the app sends this confirmation text from +1 (512) 960-3711:

Pete Ryan Slack Alerts: You're subscribed to SMS alerts for new Slack DMs and @mentions. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel.

Example alert:

Pete Ryan Slack Alerts: New Slack DM from [Sender Name]: "[First 100 characters of message]". Reply STOP to opt out.

  • Program: Pete Ryan Slack Alerts — one-way, automated notifications about Slack DMs and @mentions. No marketing or promotional messages.
  • Who receives texts: only the person who connected their own Slack account and opted in on this screen. This is a single-user app; no lists are bought, shared, or imported.
  • Frequency: varies with Slack activity.
  • Cost: Msg & data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: reply STOP (or UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT) to stop all texts. Reply START to resubscribe.
  • Help: reply HELP or email pete@feritti.vc.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
