Privacy Policy

Pete Ryan Slack Alerts · Operated by Pete Ryan (Sole Proprietor)
Effective date: September 23, 2026 · Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pete Ryan, a sole proprietor (“I,” “me,” or “Pete Ryan Slack Alerts”), collects, uses, and protects information in connection with Pete Ryan Slack Alerts, a single-user software application that sends automated SMS alerts about new Slack direct messages and @mentions to the mobile number of the user who opts in.

1. No sharing of mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories of sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. I do not sell, rent, or trade mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in data, or SMS consent.

2. Who the Service is for

Pete Ryan Slack Alerts is a single-user application. SMS alerts are sent only to the person who connected their own Slack account to the app and opted in on the app’s SMS Alert Settings screen (a public copy is at optin.html). No phone numbers are purchased, imported, or collected from anyone else.

3. Information I collect

  • Mobile phone number and SMS consent record (date/time the consent box was checked) entered on the SMS Alert Settings screen.
  • Slack message details that trigger an alert, such as the sender’s display name, the channel or conversation name, and a short preview of the message text.
  • Delivery metadata such as timestamps, message status (sent, delivered, failed), and provider message IDs from Slack and Twilio.
  • Configuration data such as Slack app tokens, Twilio account identifiers, and alert preferences needed to run the app.

4. How I use information

  • To detect new Slack DMs and @mentions and send the opted-in user an SMS alert about them.
  • To send the opt-in confirmation text and respond to HELP, STOP, and START replies.
  • To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app.
  • To comply with carrier, A2P 10DLC, and legal requirements.

I do not use this information for advertising, marketing, or promotional messages.

5. SMS program disclosures

  • Program name: Pete Ryan Slack Alerts. Messages are sent from +1 (512) 960-3711.
  • Message frequency varies with Slack activity.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time; reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP for help, or email pete@feritti.vc.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

6. Service providers

I use a small number of service providers solely to operate the app: Slack (source of the messages that trigger alerts), Twilio (SMS delivery), and hosting/compute providers that run the app. These providers process data only on my behalf to deliver the Service, and never receive mobile opt-in data or consent for their own marketing. I may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, or security.

7. Data retention

I keep message previews and delivery logs only as long as needed to deliver alerts and troubleshoot, then delete them. Consent records are kept as long as required to demonstrate compliance. If you reply STOP, your number is marked opted out and no further alerts are sent.

8. Security

I use reasonable measures to protect tokens and message data, including restricted access and encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) transport. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time; the “Last updated” date above will change when I do.
