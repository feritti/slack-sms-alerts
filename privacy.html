Privacy Policy

Slack SMS Alerts · Pete Ryan (Sole Proprietor)
Effective date: September 23, 2026 · Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Pete Ryan (“I,” “me,” or “the Service”), a sole proprietor, collects, uses, and shares information in connection with the personal Slack-to-SMS alert tool that forwards certain Slack direct messages and @mentions to my own mobile phone via SMS.

1. Who this Service is for

The Service is for my personal use only. It is not offered to the public as a multi-user product. SMS messages are sent only to my own phone number that I designate.

2. Information I process

Depending on how the Service is configured, I may process:

  • Slack message content that triggers an alert (for example, text of a DM or @mention, channel or conversation identifiers, and sender display name or user ID).
  • Phone numbers used to deliver SMS (my destination number) and the Twilio phone number used to send SMS.
  • Technical and delivery metadata such as timestamps, message status (sent, delivered, failed), and provider message identifiers from Slack and Twilio.
  • Account and configuration data such as Slack workspace app tokens, Twilio account identifiers, and routing settings I store to run the Service.

3. How I use information

I use this information only to:

  • Detect Slack DMs and @mentions that should trigger an alert.
  • Format and send SMS notifications to my designated phone number.
  • Operate, debug, and improve reliability of the Service for my own use.
  • Comply with carrier, messaging, and legal requirements (including A2P 10DLC registration).

4. SMS and messaging disclosures

  • Message frequency varies based on Slack activity (typically low volume for personal alerts).
  • Message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier.
  • You can stop SMS from this Service by replying STOP to the Twilio number. For help, reply HELP or email the contact below.
  • Because this Service is personal, the only expected SMS recipient is me. I do not sell or rent phone numbers or message content.

5. Sharing of information

I share information only with service providers needed to run the Service, including:

  • Slack — source of messages that trigger alerts.
  • Twilio — SMS delivery and related messaging compliance.
  • Hosting or compute providers I use to run the app (if any).

I do not sell personal information. I may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, or security.

6. Data retention

I retain message content and logs only as long as needed to deliver alerts, troubleshoot issues, and meet legal or carrier requirements, then delete or anonymize them when no longer needed.

7. Security

I use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect credentials and message data (for example, restricting access to tokens and preferring secure transport). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when I do. Continued use of the Service after an update means the updated policy applies.
