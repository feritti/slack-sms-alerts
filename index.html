Pete Ryan Slack Alerts

Operated by Pete Ryan (Sole Proprietor) · Austin, TX · pete@feritti.vc

Pete Ryan Slack Alerts is a single-user Slack-to-SMS notification app. When the Slack account connected to the app receives a new direct message or @mention, the app sends a short SMS alert to the mobile number of the user who opted in, so they know someone is trying to reach them while they are away from their computer.

  • Automated, one-way alerts only. No marketing or promotional messages.
  • Texts are sent from +1 (512) 960-3711 only after the user opts in with an unchecked-by-default consent box.
  • Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel.
